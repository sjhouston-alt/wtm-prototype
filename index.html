About This Experience

A five course tasting menu built around peak seasonal California ingredients, prepared in your home by a private chef. The evening begins with a welcome canape and glass of something bright, moves through a chilled crudo, a pasta course, the main, and closes with a dessert plated tableside.

Every course is introduced, every question answered. The table is set, the kitchen is left spotless, and the whole night feels like dining at a restaurant you can't get a reservation at, except the dining room is your home.

Your Host
MR
Chef Marco Rivera
Private Chef · 12 years experience
★ 4.9 rating 340+ dinners Verified
Marco trained at two Michelin starred kitchens in New York before moving west to focus on private dining. His cooking pulls from California's farmers markets with technique grounded in classical French and modern Italian. Known for warmth at the table and a calm, generous kitchen presence. Off duty, he's usually at the farmers market or surfing in El Porto.
Tonight's Menu
Five courses, paced over two and a half hours at the table.
COURSE ONE
Welcome Canape
Smoked trout on potato crisp, crème fraîche, dill
COURSE TWO
Hamachi Crudo

COURSE THREE
Hand Cut Tagliatelle

COURSE FOUR
Dry Aged Ribeye
Bone marrow jus, charred leek, roasted shallot
COURSE FIVE
Basque Cheesecake
Burnt top, cultured cream, macerated berries
